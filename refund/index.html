Refund policy

Last updated 30 October 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how the store works, not legal advice.

The promise

Do the first week. If it has not given you a clear next step, email me within 14 days of paying and I will refund you. This covers the Tracker and Guided help.

What the plans cost

  • The Tracker: first 7 days for $1, then $24 a month unless you cancel. Cancel in one click.
  • Tracker for life: $149, paid once.
  • Guided help: $75 a month for 3 months.

The quiz and the result shown on screen are free.

How to ask

Email me, or reply to any email I have sent you, within 14 days of the payment you want refunded. Message me on Threads at @yashujambu if you cannot find the email. Give the email address you paid with. There is no form and you do not need to justify it beyond saying it did not give you a clear next step.

Cancelling

You can cancel the Tracker at any time in one click. Cancelling stops future charges; it does not by itself refund a payment already taken, so ask for the refund within the 14 days if you want one.
