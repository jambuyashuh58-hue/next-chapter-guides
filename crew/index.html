    Four steps, every month

    Small, public commitments are easier to keep. The crew is there to cheer, share what worked and keep each other going.

    1

    Pick your quest

    One goal for the month in any area of life. Make it small enough to finish in 30 days.

    2

    Share it in Discord

    Post your quest in the crew, follow other people's, and swap tips and resources.

    3

    Log a check-in

    Use the monthly check-in form below: progress, one win, one blocker, next step.

    4

    Monthly Zoom

    Once a month we meet on Zoom to share updates and set the next quest. Members get the invite by email.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it free?

    Yes. The crew, the Discord and the monthly Zoom are free.

    When is the monthly Zoom?

    Once a month. The date and link go out by email and in the Discord a week before.

    Do I need Discord?

    It's where the crew chats day to day, so it's the best way to take part. If you'd rather not, you can still check in here and join the Zoom.

    What do you do with my email?

    It's only used for the monthly Zoom invite and crew updates. It's never sold or shared. Reply to any email to leave.

    Where do the career guides fit in?

    They're my own October quest. If your quest is a career change, the Next Chapter guides and the free "Is It Too Late?" calculator might help.
