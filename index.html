THE PROBLEM

Most job advice treats “I hate my job” as one problem.

So you are told to quit and follow your passion, or to stay and push through, or to ask for a raise. But hating the people, the work, the pay and hours, and being plain exhausted need four different fixes. Quit over the wrong one and you can end up in the same misery with a new logo on your badge.
